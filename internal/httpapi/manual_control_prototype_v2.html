WHAT TO CHECK

  1. 01In Automatic there are no switches at all — just what the pool is doing now and what the schedules do next.
  2. 02Switch to Manual. The switches appear and nothing is sent yet — the draft bar says so.
  3. 03Turn Filter off. The tile goes hollow and grey instantly. The amber corner dot only means it differs from the pool right now — the chips below spell out every change in words.
  4. 04Turn Heater on. Filter comes back on by itself and the line under the icons says why.
  5. 05Turn Power off. Everything follows it down. Turn Power back on — nothing else comes back.
  6. 06Apply. Watch each field go PENDING → CONFIRMED, then the countdown run.
  7. 07Arm Heater command fails in the console, apply again, then use Retry.
  8. 08Arm Pool unreachable and Observed state changed to see both refusals.
  9. 09Edit a running session — the duration restarts on apply.
  10. 10Go offline: manual setup is blocked, but Return to Automatic still works.
